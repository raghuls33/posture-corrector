What it is

A wearable posture device paired with a mobile app. It senses when your head and neck drift forward and nudges you back to a healthy position.

How it works

  1. 01

    Wear & pair

    Put on the device and connect it to the mobile app.

  2. 02

    Detect

    Sensors pick up forward-neck posture as it happens.

  3. 03

    Nudge

    A gentle vibration prompts you to correct your position.

  4. 04

    Track

    The app records posture data so conditions and progress can be monitored over time.

Key features

Forward-neck detection

Senses when your head drifts ahead of your shoulders.

Gentle vibration feedback

A quiet buzz you feel, without sound or a screen.

Mobile app tracking

Posture history and progress monitoring in one place.

Who it's for

Office workers Students Long hours on phones Long hours on computers Physiotherapy patients
